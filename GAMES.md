# RE:PLAY — Game Roadmap

This document tracks current games for RE:PLAY.

---

## ✅ Core Games (Launch Targets)

### Paddle Duel
- Physics-based ball movement
- AI and local multiplayer
- Speed ramps and optional modifiers

### Brickfall
- Destructible brick patterns
- Powerups (multi-ball, wide paddle, slow time)
- Level progression

### Void Drift
- Thrust-based ship movement
- Screen wrapping
- Procedural asteroid splitting
---

## ❌ Explicit Non-Goals
- No emulation
- No ROM loading
- No copyrighted assets
- No online multiplayer (for now)
